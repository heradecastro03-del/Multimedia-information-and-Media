📺 Types of Media

Media is an important part of everyday life. It helps people to communicate,share ideas and information, and stay updated on news, education, and entertainment.

📰 Print Media

Print media is information printed on paper.

  • Newspaper
  • Magazine
  • Books
  • Brochures

📻 Broadcast Media

Broadcast media delivers information through television and radio.

  • Radio
  • Television
  • News Programs
  • Podcasts

💻 Online Media

Online media uses the internet to share information quickly with people.

  • Facebook
  • YouTube
  • Instagram
  • Websites
